C-Cord is a fast, lightweight native Discord client written in C23 with SDL3.

Highlights:
• Native responsive mobile UI with drag-scroll & kinetic fling, long-press bottom sheets, slide-out navigation & member drawers, and edge-to-edge safe-area / soft-keyboard handling.
• Voice channels with Opus audio, DAVE E2EE, MediaCodec hardware-accelerated video, camera & MediaProjection screen sharing, and foreground call notification controls.
• Built-in Dark, Light, and AMOLED themes plus QuickCSS customization.
• Sandboxed Lua 5.5 plugin runtime (Plugin API v1) with cross-platform OS/capability detection and native mobile haptics & share sheet support.
