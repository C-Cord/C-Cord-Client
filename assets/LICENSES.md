The license is read from the font's own `name` table (IDs 0, 13, 14). The Arch package's

The two `*-Subset.ttf` fallback fonts are disk-bundled only (too large to embed in the binary) and are loaded
before any system fonts. Their `name` tables keep the original copyright and OFL text (IDs 0, 13, 14):
`NotoColorEmoji-Subset.ttf` is "Copyright 2022 Google Inc." and `NotoSansCJK-Subset.ttf` is
"© 2014-2021 Adobe (http://www.adobe.com/)". Regenerate with
`python3 scripts/subset_fallback_fonts.py` (needs fontTools, the two host fonts above and
`/usr/share/i18n/charmaps/GB2312.gz`); the emoji subset keeps every code point of the emoji data table
plus ZWJ/skin-tone/regional-indicator/tag helpers, the CJK subset keeps GB2312, kana, CJK punctuation
and the full Hangul syllable block.

## Icons

UI glyphs in `src/ui/icons.c` are procedural. The C-Cord application and tray icons in `assets/icons/` (and embedded in `src/platform/app_icon_data.c`) are original project artwork distributed under the repository's AGPL-3.0 license (no Discord assets used).

## Bundled extension template

`extensions/starter-example/` is licensed under the **MIT License** so plugin authors may freely copy and adapt it for open-source or proprietary extensions alongside [`LICENSES/C-Cord-Extension-Exception.txt`](../LICENSES/C-Cord-Extension-Exception.txt).

## Themes

`themes/c-cord-dark.theme`, `themes/c-cord-light.theme` and `themes/c-cord-amoled.theme` hold original colour
values designed for C-Cord's dark ("Matte Graphite"), light ("Warm Stone") and pure-black ("Obsidian") palettes.
They contain no third-party assets.
